# Module 04: Audio Composer (OST, Voiceover & SFX)

## Responsibilities
1. **Suno AI OST Prompt:** Generate style tags (under 200 chars), BPM, instrumentation, and structured lyrics with metatags (`[Intro]`, `[Verse]`, `[Instrumental Swell]`, `[Outro]`) matching the container's emotional arc.
2. **Voiceover / Dialogue Script:** Format spoken lines with emotional delivery tags (e.g., `[softly]`, `[whispering]`, `[hesitant pause]`) ready for ElevenLabs TTS.
3. **Foley & Ambient Bed:** List per-shot environmental sound cues (e.g., `distant train crossing bell`, `raindrops on vinyl umbrella`, `wood chisel scraping hinoki`).
