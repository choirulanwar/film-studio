# Module 02: Art Department (Character Sheets & Location Plates)

## Responsibilities
Generate clean, neutral reference assets before scene photography begins to prevent visual drift.

## Character Turnaround Sheet Rules
- **Composition:** Full-body multi-view turnaround sheet (front view, side profile, back view, plus close-up facial expression grid) on a solid neutral light-gray studio background.
- **Lighting:** Flat, even studio lighting. **FORBIDDEN:** dramatic rim lights, heavy film grain, mist diffusion, or shallow bokeh (these distort facial geometry and outfit details).
- **Prompt Template:**
  `Character turnaround sheet of [AGE, ETHNICITY, HAIRSTYLE, FACIAL FEATURES], wearing [EXACT LOCKED WARDROBE], showing front view, side profile, back view, and three facial expressions, standing in neutral A-pose, solid neutral light-gray background, even soft studio lighting, crisp garment details, clean concept art reference sheet --ar 16:9`

## Location Clean Plate Rules
- **Composition:** Empty architectural or environmental establishing plate with **zero people** in frame.
- **Prompt Template:**
  `Empty establishing background plate of [LOCATION DETAILS, ARCHITECTURE, PROPS], [BASE TIME OF DAY & WEATHER], eye-level wide architectural framing, no people, clean spatial layout, [LOCKED LENS PRESET] --ar [PROJECT AR]`
