# Reference: Color Grading & Film Stock Presets

Select **ONE** preset per project and lock its `preset_id` in `bible.json`.

## 1. `jp-melancholic-rainy` (Japanese Indie 35mm Overcast)
- **Best For:** Rainy scenes, quiet introspection, urban/rural melancholy.
- **Characteristics:** Desaturated slate-blue and muted teal environment, lifted milky shadows, soft rolled-off highlights, low contrast, selective vibrant accent (e.g., red umbrella).
- **Style Suffix:** `shot on 35mm analog film, Japanese indie cinema aesthetic, melancholic rainy overcast atmosphere, desaturated slate-blue and muted teal color grading, lifted milky shadows, soft rolled-off highlights, low contrast, natural diffused lighting, Fujifilm Superia 400 look, fine film grain`

## 2. `jp-airy-portra-overexposed` (Dreamy Soft-Focus Daylight)
- **Best For:** Slice-of-life, gentle romance, morning/autumn daylight.
- **Characteristics:** High-key overexposed +1 stop, heavy Black Pro-Mist bloom, creamy ivory and muted beige tones, warm lifted shadows, ultra-low contrast.
- **Style Suffix:** `Japanese airy photography, Kodak Portra 400 overexposed +1 stop, heavy Black Pro-Mist diffusion filter, dreamy soft focus, blooming highlights, creamy ivory and muted beige color palette, warm lifted shadows, ultra-low contrast, translucent Japanese magazine aesthetic, fine analog film grain`

## 3. `jp-cyan-winter-daylight` (Cool Cerulean & Soft Peach)
- **Best For:** Train stations, winter/spring commutes, crisp nostalgic daylight.
- **Characteristics:** Cool cerulean/slate-blue shadows paired with clean peachy-pink skin tones, low-to-medium contrast, airy highlights.
- **Style Suffix:** `Japanese airy photography, cool cerulean blue and soft peach color grading, Fujifilm Classic Chrome look, lifted blue-tinted shadows, clean bright highlights, low contrast, diffused winter daylight, soft film grain`

## 4. `jp-sun-drenched-portrait` (Dappled Golden Sunlight & Blue Shadows)
- **Best For:** Intimate close-ups, warm outdoor moments, golden hour foliage.
- **Characteristics:** Warm golden sunlight creating hair rim-light against cool blue-tinted concrete/wall shadows, soft halation, translucent skin glow.
- **Style Suffix:** `Japanese sun-drenched portrait photography, Kodak Portra 400 film aesthetic, warm sunlight with cool blue shadows, soft blooming highlights, dreamy Black Pro-Mist diffusion glow, translucent porcelain-peach skin tones, dappled tree shadows, fine analog film grain`

## 5. `jp-blue-hour-cinestill` (Twilight Teal & Warm Tungsten)
- **Best For:** Dusk, night streets, convenience stores, emotional winter evenings.
- **Characteristics:** Deep navy/slate-blue twilight shadows contrasting with warm golden tungsten interior lights or sunset rim-light, rich cinematic contrast, subtle halation.
- **Style Suffix:** `Japanese blue hour cinema photography, CineStill 800T film aesthetic, deep cool slate-blue shadows contrasting with warm golden tungsten glow, rich cinematic contrast, twilight sky gradient, subtle halation around lights, fine 35mm film grain`

## 6. `neutral-log-base` (Clean Base for External Grading)
- **Best For:** Maximum character consistency when color grading will be applied manually in DaVinci Resolve.
- **Style Suffix:** `natural balanced film photography, Kodak Vision3 250D neutral color profile, accurate skin tones, wide dynamic range, soft natural lighting, minimal color cast, fine 35mm film grain`
