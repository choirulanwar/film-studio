VIDEO STYLE & FORMAT
20-second scene in the style of a modern Indonesian short drama. ONE continuous
arrival — a single unbroken moment at one house gate, NOT a trailer, NOT a montage —
covered like a real film: cut between MULTIPLE camera positions, angles changing
every few seconds while the moment flows continuously. Slow, patient pacing with
real silence. Naturalistic acting, underplayed, never theatrical. Look: warm
tropical daylight, honest midday sun, saturated greens and warm skin tones, fine
film grain. This is a quiet everyday drama, NOT horror and NOT a thriller.

All spoken dialogue in INDONESIAN (Bahasa Indonesia), everyday casual spoken form —
