# Workflow: Music Video (MV) & Song Visualizer

## Target Specifications
- **Default Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` (YouTube/Bilibili) or `9:16` (Shorts/Reels)
- **Container Structure:** Single Track mapped to song sections (`Intro`, `Verse 1`, `Chorus 1`, `Verse 2`, `Chorus 2`, `Bridge`, `Outro`)

## Audio-Visual Sync Rules
1. **Lyrics-First Mapping:** If lyrics or Suno metatags exist, map every visual beat directly to lyrical imagery and emotional dynamics.
2. **Chorus Escalation:** Reserve dynamic camera movements (`dolly-in`, `crane`, `tracking`) and richer lighting contrasts for Chorus and Bridge sections; use static or gentle atmospheric motion for Verses.
3. **Loop-Friendly Segments:** Ensure each shot prompt maintains consistent subject positioning so clips can be cross-dissolved cleanly.
